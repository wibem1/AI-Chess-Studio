This folder contains ONNX conversions of the Maia-2 model weights ("rapid" and
"blitz" checkpoints) and a TypeScript re-implementation of Maia-2's input
encoding and output decoding. Maia-2 is by CSSLab, University of Toronto
(https://github.com/CSSLab/maia2), distributed under the MIT License below.
Any redistribution of the models/*.onnx files (including inside an app bundle)
must keep this notice.

Paper: Tang, Jiao, McIlroy-Young, Kleinberg, Sen, Anderson. "Maia-2: A Unified
Model for Human-AI Alignment in Chess". NeurIPS 2024. arXiv:2409.20553.
